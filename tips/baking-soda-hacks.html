10 Cleaning Hacks with Baking Soda You Wish You Knew Sooner

Baking soda is the quiet hero of the cleaning cupboard: cheap, gentle and surprisingly powerful. It lifts odors, cuts grease and scrubs without scratching. Here are ten ways to put one box to work all over the house.

What you need

  • Baking soda (one box goes a long way)
  • White vinegar
  • An old toothbrush
  • Microfiber cloths and a sponge
  • A spray bottle

How to do it

  1. Freshen the sink drain: pour 1/2 cup baking soda down the drain, follow with 1 cup warm vinegar, wait 10 minutes, then flush with hot water.
  2. Deodorize the fridge: leave an open box of baking soda on a shelf. Replace it every month.
  3. Scrub the stovetop: make a paste of 3 parts baking soda to 1 part water, spread it on, wait 15 minutes, scrub and wipe.
  4. Clean the microwave: microwave a bowl of water with 2 tablespoons baking soda for 2 minutes, then wipe the softened grime away.
  5. Brighten grout: mix baking soda with a little water into a paste, scrub grout lines with an old toothbrush, rinse.
  6. Deodorize carpets: sprinkle baking soda over the carpet, wait 15–30 minutes, vacuum thoroughly.
  7. Freshen sneakers: shake a spoonful into each shoe, leave overnight, shake out in the morning.
  8. Clean the cutting board: sprinkle baking soda on a damp board, scrub, rinse, and let it dry standing up.
  9. Shine the stainless steel sink: sprinkle baking soda, scrub gently with a damp cloth, rinse, and buff dry.
  10. Deodorize the trash can: sprinkle baking soda in the bottom of the can under the bag — it absorbs odors for weeks.
Good to know: Baking soda is a mild abrasive, so it is safe on most surfaces — but skip it on aluminum, which it can discolor, and always test a small hidden spot first on delicate finishes.

What to buy

  • Baking soda, multi-pack
  • Microfiber cloth set
  • Grout brush
