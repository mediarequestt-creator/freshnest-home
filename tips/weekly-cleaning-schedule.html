Weekly Cleaning Schedule

The weekly schedule is the secret behind every "always clean" home: one small job per day, 15–20 minutes each, so nothing ever builds up into a weekend project. Print it, stick it on the fridge, and follow it for two weeks — it becomes automatic.

What you need

  • This schedule (save or print the page)
  • Basic supplies: all-purpose cleaner, microfiber cloths, vacuum, mop
  • A laundry basket for clutter

The schedule

  1. Monday — Kitchen reset: wipe all counters and appliances, clean the stovetop, take out the trash, and wipe the sink.
  2. Tuesday — Bathrooms: clean the toilets, sinks and mirrors, wipe the shower, and hang fresh towels.
  3. Wednesday — Dusting: dust shelves, picture frames, lamps and electronics, top to bottom, with a damp microfiber cloth.
  4. Thursday — Floors: vacuum all rooms, then mop the kitchen and bathrooms.
  5. Friday — Laundry and linens: wash bedding and towels, fold and put away everything that is dry.
  6. Saturday — One deep job: pick one rotating job — fridge, oven, windows, or a closet. Just one.
  7. Sunday — Rest and reset: a 15-minute whole-house tidy, take out the trash, and set out clean towels for Monday.
Good to know: Miss a day? Just skip it and do it next week — never try to catch up by doubling a day, or the schedule stops feeling doable. The daily 15-minute routine handles the basics in between.
