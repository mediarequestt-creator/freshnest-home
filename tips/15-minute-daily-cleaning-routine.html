15-Minute Daily Cleaning Routine

Fifteen minutes a day is all it takes to keep a home from sliding into chaos. The routine is short on purpose: five small jobs, same time every day, so it becomes a habit instead of a chore.

What you need

  • A timer (your phone works fine)
  • A laundry basket for quick clutter sweeps
  • Microfiber cloth and all-purpose cleaner
  • A small vacuum or broom

How to do it

  1. Minutes 0–3 — Make the beds: a made bed makes the whole bedroom look finished.
  2. Minutes 3–6 — Clutter sweep: carry the basket through the main rooms and collect everything that is out of place. Put it all away at the end.
  3. Minutes 6–9 — Kitchen reset: load the dishwasher, wipe the counters and sink, hang a fresh towel.
  4. Minutes 9–12 — Bathroom wipe: quick wipe of the sink, mirror and toilet seat. Done daily, it never needs a deep scrub.
  5. Minutes 12–15 — Floors: sweep or vacuum the high-traffic areas — entryway, kitchen, living room.
Good to know: Pick a fixed time — right after breakfast, or right after dinner — and set a timer. When the timer rings, stop. A routine you actually finish beats a perfect routine you quit after three days.

What to buy

  • Microfiber cloth set
  • Lightweight cordless vacuum
  • Storage basket for clutter sweeps
