DIY Natural All-Purpose Cleaner (3 Ingredients)

Store-bought sprays work, but this homemade version costs almost nothing, takes two minutes to mix, and handles most everyday messes — kitchen counters, bathroom sinks, light switches and more.

What you need

  • 1 cup white vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon dish soap
  • A clean spray bottle
  • Optional: a few drops of lemon or lavender essential oil for scent

How to do it

  1. Mix: pour the water into the spray bottle first, then add the vinegar and dish soap. Add essential oil if you like.
  2. Shake gently: swirl the bottle to combine — do not shake hard or it will foam up.
  3. Label it: write the contents and the date on the bottle. It keeps for about a month.
  4. Spray and wipe: spray the surface, wait 30 seconds, and wipe with a microfiber cloth. For sticky spots, let it sit for a minute first.
  5. Use it on: counters, sinks, faucets, stovetops, appliances, light switches, door handles and trash can lids.
Good to know: The dish soap is what cuts grease — vinegar alone does not. A tiny amount is enough; more soap just leaves a film you have to rinse off.
Safety first: Do not use this vinegar cleaner on natural stone (marble, granite), hardwood floors, or waxed surfaces — vinegar can etch stone and dull finishes. Never add bleach to this or any homemade cleaner.

What to buy

  • Glass spray bottles, 2-pack
  • Microfiber cloth set
  • White vinegar, gallon size
