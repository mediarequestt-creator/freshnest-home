Laundry Room Organization for Small Spaces

A small laundry room does not have to feel cramped. The fix is almost always the same: get things off the floor, use the wall, and keep only the products you actually use. Here is how to set it up so laundry day feels easy.

What you need

  • Two or three sorting hampers or bins
  • Wall hooks or a small shelf above the machines
  • Clear bins or jars for detergent, pods and stain remover
  • Labels (or masking tape and a marker)
  • A fold-down drying rack if space allows

How to do it

  1. Empty it completely: take everything out and throw away dried-out products and mystery bottles.
  2. Sort by default: set up three hampers — lights, darks, towels — so sorting happens at the hamper, not on laundry day.
  3. Use the wall: put a shelf or a row of hooks above the washer and dryer for detergent, stain remover and the iron.
  4. Decant the essentials: pour detergent and pods into clear jars or bins so you can see at a glance what is running low.
  5. Label everything: every bin gets a label. If it has no label, it has no home — and it will end up back on the floor.
  6. Add a folding surface: a board over the machines or a small wall-mounted shelf gives you a place to fold.
  7. Keep a lost-sock basket: one small basket for single socks ends the hunt forever.
Good to know: The one-in, one-out rule keeps small laundry rooms from filling up again: every time a new product comes in, an old or empty one goes out.

What to buy

  • Laundry sorter with 3 compartments
  • Stackable storage bins
  • Wall-mounted drying rack
