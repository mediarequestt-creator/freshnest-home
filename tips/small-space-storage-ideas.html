Small-Space Storage Ideas for Every Room

Small homes do not need more stuff — they need smarter places to put the stuff they already have. These ideas use walls, doors and forgotten corners, room by room, so everything has a home without buying bigger furniture.

What you need

  • Stackable bins and clear containers
  • Command hooks and over-door racks
  • A tension rod or two
  • Drawer dividers
  • Labels

How to do it

  1. Kitchen: hang a rail with hooks for utensils, use a tension rod under the sink to hang spray bottles, and stack bins inside deep cabinets so nothing gets lost at the back.
  2. Bathroom: put a small shelf above the door, use over-door hooks for towels, and keep daily items in one caddy that lives under the sink.
  3. Bedroom: store out-of-season clothes in flat bins under the bed, and use drawer dividers so one drawer holds what three used to.
  4. Closet: add a second hanging rod below the first for shirts, use shelf dividers to stop stacks from toppling, and hang a shoe organizer on the door for accessories too.
  5. Entryway: one hook per person, one small basket for keys and mail, and a shoe tray by the door — the whole room is one square meter, but it works.
  6. Everywhere: use the inside of cabinet doors — spice racks, cleaning supply holders and measuring cups all fit there.
Good to know: Measure before you buy anything. The most common small-space mistake is buying a "perfect" bin that is half an inch too wide for the shelf it was meant for.

What to buy

  • Stackable storage bins, set of 4
  • Over-door hooks and rack
  • Drawer dividers, adjustable
