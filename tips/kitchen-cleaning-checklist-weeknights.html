Kitchen Cleaning Checklist for Busy Weeknights

The kitchen is the room that falls apart fastest — and the one that feels best when it is clean. This 10-minute checklist, done right after dinner, keeps it under control all week so the weekend never turns into a rescue mission.

What you need

  • Dish soap and a sponge
  • All-purpose cleaner and a microfiber cloth
  • A small trash bag for a quick sweep of expired items
  • A clean dish towel

How to do it

  1. Clear the table and counters: put everything away — nothing lives on the counter overnight.
  2. Load the dishwasher: scrape plates, load dishes, and start the cycle. Hand-wash what does not fit.
  3. Wipe the stovetop: while it is still slightly warm (not hot), wipe spills before they harden.
  4. Clean the sink: rinse it out, scrub with a little dish soap, and dry it — a dry sink stays cleaner.
  5. Wipe the counters: one pass with all-purpose cleaner, including around the coffee maker and toaster.
  6. Take out the trash: if the bin is more than half full, take it out now so it never smells in the morning.
  7. Shake the rug and sweep: a quick sweep of crumbs takes one minute and changes everything.
  8. Hang a fresh towel: small thing, big difference — the kitchen looks finished.
Good to know: The secret is the "clean as you cook" habit: wash the cutting board and wipe the counter while food is on the stove, and the after-dinner checklist shrinks to five minutes.

What to buy
