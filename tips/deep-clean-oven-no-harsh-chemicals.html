How to Deep Clean Your Oven Without Harsh Chemicals

Oven cleaner sprays work, but the fumes are brutal. This baking soda method takes longer — mostly waiting — but there are no harsh chemicals, no gloves full of warnings, and the oven comes out just as clean.

What you need

  • Baking soda (about 1/2 cup)
  • Water
  • White vinegar in a spray bottle
  • A spatula or old credit card for scraping
  • Microfiber cloths and a sponge

How to do it

  1. Empty the oven: take out the racks and set them aside. Wipe out loose crumbs with a dry cloth.
  2. Make the paste: mix 1/2 cup baking soda with 3 tablespoons water into a spreadable paste.
  3. Coat the inside: spread the paste over the oven walls, floor and door — skip the heating elements and the fan. Wear gloves; it is messy but harmless.
  4. Wait overnight: leave the paste for at least 12 hours. The baking soda softens baked-on grease while you sleep.
  5. Soak the racks: while you wait, soak the racks in the bathtub or a large sink with hot water and a few tablespoons of dish soap.
  6. Wipe it out: the next day, scrape off the dried paste with a spatula, then wipe with a damp cloth until clean.
  7. Spray with vinegar: spray the inside lightly with vinegar to lift the last residue, wipe once more, and dry.
  8. Replace the racks: scrub the soaked racks, rinse, dry and slide them back in.
Good to know: The fizz when vinegar meets baking soda looks impressive, but it mostly just neutralizes both — the real cleaning was the overnight paste. The vinegar spray at the end just helps lift the last film.
Safety first: Make sure the oven is completely cool before you start, and keep the paste off the heating elements. Never use oven cleaner sprays and baking soda paste in the same session, and never run the self-cleaning cycle with any paste or cleaner inside.
