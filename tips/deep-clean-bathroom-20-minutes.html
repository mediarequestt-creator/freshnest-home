How to Deep Clean Your Bathroom in 20 Minutes

A bathroom deep clean sounds like a weekend project, but with the right order it takes about 20 minutes. The trick is to let the cleaners do the work while you move on to the next job — no scrubbing the same spot twice.

What you need

  • All-purpose cleaner or bathroom cleaner
  • Toilet bowl cleaner and a toilet brush
  • Glass cleaner and a microfiber cloth
  • An old toothbrush for edges and grout
  • Rubber gloves

How to do it

  1. Minutes 0–2 — Clear and spray: take everything off the counters, spray the shower, tub and toilet bowl with cleaner, and let it sit.
  2. Minutes 2–6 — Shower and tub: while the cleaner works, scrub the shower walls, tub and faucet. Rinse.
  3. Minutes 6–10 — Toilet: scrub the bowl with the brush, wipe the seat, lid and base with a cloth. Wash your hands after.
  4. Minutes 10–14 — Sink and counters: scrub the sink and faucet, wipe the counter, and clean around the soap dish and toothbrush holder.
  5. Minutes 14–17 — Mirror and fixtures: spray glass cleaner on the mirror and wipe with a dry microfiber cloth. Buff the faucets.
  6. Minutes 17–20 — Floor and finish: sweep or vacuum, mop the floor, hang fresh towels, and put everything back.
Good to know: Always clean top to bottom and let sprays sit for a few minutes before scrubbing — the dwell time does half the work for you. Keep the fan on or a window open while you clean.
Safety first: Never mix bleach with vinegar, ammonia, or other cleaners — the fumes are dangerous. Use one product at a time and rinse between products if you switch.

What to buy

  • Microfiber cloth set
  • Grout brush set
  • Toilet brush and holder
